# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Beacon serves onboard and field operators managing vessel data routing in real shipboard conditions, developers and integrators building against NMEA 2000 data flows, and maritime AI systems that need reliable context about vessel messages, routes, filters, and delivery state.

## Product Purpose

Beacon is a ship-board NMEA 2000 gateway that makes vessel data routing observable, configurable, and resilient. It lets operators and integrators connect CAN, USB-CAN, HTTP, WebSocket, SSE, TCP, and MQTT endpoints; route messages through explicit connectors; filter traffic with CEL; and keep delivery robust through durable buffering and replay.

Success means a user can understand what is connected, what is moving, what is degraded, and what to change without restarting the system or losing trust in the gateway while underway.

## Positioning

A ship-board NMEA 2000 gateway that makes vessel data routing observable, configurable, and resilient.

## Brand Personality

Quiet industrial control with enough maritime and AI context to make the system legible to humans and agents. The interface should feel precise, operational, restrained, and consistent with the Open Ships design language rather than decorative or promotional.

## Anti-references

Do not drift away from the openships.ai visual system. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, ornamental nautical styling, cloud-first positioning, heavy marketing composition, and UI treatments that make operational state harder to scan.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the routing topology visible. Source -> connector -> sink is the core mental model and should remain obvious on setup, normal operation, and failure states.
2. Prefer operational clarity over decoration. Screens should be dense enough for repeated use, with restrained color and typography that support scanning.
3. Make state explainable. Live status, queue depth, replay behavior, disabled components, and degraded routes should be surfaced in language that works for operators, developers, and AI consumers.
4. Preserve local-first confidence. The product should feel dependable aboard a vessel, including offline use, immediate config writes, and clear recovery paths.
5. Stay aligned with Open Ships. Beacon should inherit openships.ai's white surface, compact navigation, gray text system, blue accent, and practical documentation tone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion-friendly interactions, and clear status language. Accessibility work should reinforce the same quiet industrial-control feel rather than introduce separate visual conventions.
